I am a PhD scholar in the Computational Wave Mechanics Lab at the Indian Institute of Science, working with Prof. S. Gopalakrishnan. I study how elastic waves travel through engineered aerospace structures, and how to design materials that stop them. My work focuses on aluminium structures reinforced with quasicrystals, a class of materials whose extra phason field gives a new way to tune vibration and wave behaviour. I model these structures with spectral finite element analysis to predict dispersion, band gaps and dynamic response.

My path into structural mechanics began with a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering at IET Lucknow, where I built a thermoacoustic refrigerator. It continued with an M.Tech in Machine Design at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, where I studied fracture and fatigue life of hip prostheses, and led to my PhD at IISc in 2021.

Research interests: Guided wave propagation · Band-gap engineering · Quasicrystal-reinforced structures · Spectral finite element analysis · Structural health monitoring

PhD · Ongoing

Guided Waves in Quasicrystal-Reinforced Plates

Computational Wave Mechanics Lab, IISc · Advisor: Prof. S. Gopalakrishnan

Quasicrystals carry a phason field in addition to the usual elastic field. This work models how that extra field changes the way waves travel through quasicrystal plates and aluminium plates reinforced with quasicrystals. It covers guided Lamb and leaky Lamb waves, and Rayleigh and Stoneley waves at surfaces and interfaces, with spectral finite element models used to compute dispersion and dynamic response. The results support guided-wave methods for structural health monitoring.

2021 – now
PhD · Ongoing

Phason-Tunable Band Gaps for Vibration Isolation

Computational Wave Mechanics Lab, IISc

Periodic structures block waves over certain frequency ranges, called band gaps. This work asks whether a material can attenuate a broad band of frequencies by itself, without any attached active or passive vibration-control device. It studies periodic aluminium–quasicrystal structures and phononic crystals, and how the phason field shifts and widens their band gaps.

  • Band gaps
  • Phononic crystals
  • Periodic structures
  • Vibration isolation

Fracture Analysis of a Hip Prosthesis under Fatigue Loading

M.Tech in Machine Design · IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

Multi-mode fracture analysis of a Ti-6Al-4V prosthetic hip under dynamic fatigue loading. The thesis determined the critical crack size using fracture mechanics and predicted fatigue life from crack initiation to final fracture under variable loading.

B.Tech project

Standing-Wave Thermoacoustic Refrigerator

B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering · IET Lucknow

Designed and built a refrigerator that uses a standing sound wave to pump heat. The prototype achieved an 8 °C temperature difference across the stack, after optimising stack geometry and resonator length to improve the coefficient of performance.
